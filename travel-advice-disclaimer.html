1. General information only

Fieldnote Travel Pty Ltd publishes destination guides, sample itineraries, checklists and articles for a general Australian audience. This material is:

  • general in nature, and does not take account of your circumstances, your health, your passport, your finances or your travel history
  • a point-in-time snapshot, accurate to the best of our knowledge on the last-updated date shown on the page, and not maintained in real time
  • not professional advice of any kind, including financial, insurance, medical, legal, tax or migration advice
  • not a substitute for official sources, which always take precedence over anything we publish

You are responsible for verifying anything you intend to rely on. Where information matters to whether you can travel, whether you are insured, or whether you are safe, verify it with the relevant government or a qualified professional.

3. Visas and entry requirements

Where a guide describes the visa position for Australian passport holders, it describes the general position for tourism at the time of writing. It is not a determination about you.

Entry requirements change without notice, and they vary with:

  • the purpose of your travel
  • the length of your stay
  • your passport validity, commonly a minimum of six months beyond entry or departure
  • the number of blank pages in your passport
  • whether you hold dual nationality or have travel history the destination cares about
  • whether you are transiting, and whether you clear immigration to do so
  • proof of onward or return travel, and proof of funds

Airlines enforce these rules at the departure gate in Australia. If your documents do not meet the destination's requirements, you will be refused boarding and you will generally not be refunded. We are not responsible for that outcome.

If your circumstances are complex, consult the destination's embassy or a registered migration agent. We are not registered migration agents and cannot advise on visa applications.

4. Safety information and advisory levels

Safety sections in our guides summarise commonly reported risks, such as road conditions, water safety, wildlife, weather and health hazards. They are written to be practically useful, not to be exhaustive.

They are not a substitute for the current Smartraveller advisory level for the destination, which is set by the Australian Government and can change quickly in response to events. Advisory levels also matter commercially: many travel insurance policies limit or exclude cover for travel to a destination at a Do Not Travel level, and some are affected at lower levels.

Check the advisory before you book, before you depart, and while you are away if conditions change. Consider subscribing to Smartraveller updates for your destination.

5. Health, vaccination and medication

We are not medical practitioners and nothing on this site is medical advice.

Vaccination requirements and recommendations differ by destination, by region within a destination, by season and by individual medical history. Some vaccination courses require weeks to complete. Some countries require proof of vaccination as a condition of entry.

Medication rules also differ. Medicines that are ordinary and legal in Australia are restricted or prohibited in some countries, and carrying them without the right documentation can have serious consequences. Check with the destination's embassy and carry prescriptions in original packaging with a doctor's letter.

Speak to a GP or a travel health clinic at least six to eight weeks before departure.

6. Costs, prices and budgets

Every cost figure on this site is an estimate, in Australian dollars, prepared in good faith for typical mid-range travel, with the assumptions stated near the figure. Estimates are not quotes, offers, or guaranteed prices, and we do not control the prices charged by any provider.

Actual costs vary with season, day of week, exchange rate, availability, how far ahead you book, and choices you make on the day. Foreign exchange rates move, and a figure converted at one rate will be wrong at another.

Check current prices directly with the provider before committing to anything. Do not treat a Fieldnote budget table as a booking quotation.

7. Insurance

Travel insurance is a regulated financial product in Australia. Fieldnote Travel Pty Ltd is not an insurer, not an insurance broker, and does not hold an Australian Financial Services Licence.

Our Travel Insurance Guide is general information about how the product category works. It is not personal financial advice, it does not take account of your objectives, financial situation or needs, and it does not recommend any policy. Before deciding, read the Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination, and consult a licensed adviser.

8. Third-party providers and bookings

Fieldnote is not a travel agent and does not sell travel. We do not take bookings, we are not a party to any booking you make, and we do not hold your money for travel arrangements.

Where we link to an airline, hotel, car hire company, tour operator or insurer, we do not control that business, its terms, its pricing, its safety practices or its service quality. Your contract is with them. If something goes wrong with a booking, your remedy lies against that provider.

Some links are affiliate links; see the Affiliate Disclosure. A commercial arrangement is not an endorsement of a provider's safety record.

9. If you are in trouble overseas

Do not contact us. We cannot help in an emergency and we do not provide consular assistance.

  • Contact local emergency services first.
  • Contact the nearest Australian embassy, high commission or consulate.
  • The Australian Government's Consular Emergency Centre operates 24 hours a day for Australians overseas. The number is published on Smartraveller. From within Australia, family can call 1300 555 135.
  • Contact your travel insurer's 24-hour emergency assistance line before incurring major medical costs, if you are able to. Many policies require prior authorisation.

Carry these numbers written down as well as in your phone.
