2. Cancelling a subscription

Cancel from Account, then Billing, then Cancel Subscription. It takes two clicks. There is no phone call, no retention offer and no requirement to give a reason, although we would like to know if something went wrong.

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. You keep Pro access until then.
  • No further payments are taken after you cancel.
  • You will receive an email confirming the cancellation and the date access ends. If you do not receive it, contact billing@fieldnotetravel.com.au.
  • You can resubscribe at any time and your trips will still be there.

What happens to your trips

Nothing is deleted when Pro ends. If you have more than two saved trips, they become read-only until you either delete some or resubscribe. You can still view them, print them and copy the text. Export any trip you want as a PDF before Pro access ends.

3. The 14 day refund window

We offer a full refund of a Pro subscription payment where:

  1. the request is made within 14 days of the charge, and
  2. the charge is either a first purchase of Pro on that account, or an annual renewal, and
  3. you have not exported a PDF and not created a sharing link since that charge

Those two features are the substantive things Pro adds, so using them is treated as having received the benefit of the subscription. Building trips, reading guides and printing from the browser do not affect eligibility, because printing is available on the free tier.

The 14 day window applies per charge. Monthly renewals after a first purchase are not separately refundable, because you have had a month to cancel before each one and cancelling takes effect immediately for future periods.

We do not currently offer pro-rata refunds of a partly used monthly period, since you retain access for the whole period you paid for.

A change of travel plans on your part after delivery is not grounds for a refund, because the work has been performed. We will, however, adjust dates in a delivered plan once at no charge within 60 days.

5. Business and white-label plans

  • Agent and Agency plans may be cancelled with 30 days' written notice to partners@fieldnotetravel.com.au. The current month is not refunded; no charge is raised for months following the notice period.
  • Seats added mid-cycle are charged pro-rata. Seats removed mid-cycle take effect at the next billing date and are not refunded for the current month.
  • White-label plans have a minimum initial term stated in the order form, typically six months, because of the build work involved. Setup and custom build fees are non-refundable once work has commenced.
  • Data on termination. You may export your client itineraries at any time. We retain your account data for 30 days after termination so you can retrieve it, then delete it on request or automatically after 90 days.

6. Failed payments

If a payment fails we retry it, and we email you. If it is still unpaid after 14 days the account reverts to the free tier automatically. We do not send accounts to debt collection over a consumer subscription, and we do not charge late fees.

Your trips are not deleted when this happens. The read-only rule in section 2 applies.

7. How refunds are processed

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or to a bank account.
  • We approve or decline within 5 business days of receiving a request, and tell you which.
  • Approved refunds are submitted within 10 business days of approval.
  • Your bank or card issuer then takes its own time, commonly 3 to 10 business days, to show the credit. That part is outside our control.
  • Refunds are made in Australian dollars, for the amount charged including GST. If your card is denominated in another currency, the amount you receive may differ because of exchange rate movement or your issuer's fees. We cannot compensate for that difference.
  • An adjustment note is issued for GST purposes.

8. Requests outside the standard window

The window is a stated policy, not a legal maximum, and we will consider requests outside it on their merits. Circumstances where we have refunded outside the window include:

  • a duplicate charge, or a charge on an account the holder did not authorise
  • a renewal charged after a cancellation was submitted but not processed
  • a significant outage that prevented use of a paid feature
  • a paid feature being withdrawn during a period already paid for
  • bereavement, serious illness or similar circumstances, on request and without requiring evidence

Ask. The worst outcome is that we explain why not.

9. Your Australian Consumer Law rights

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law, in Schedule 2 to the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth). Services must be supplied with due care and skill, be fit for any purpose we advertised, and be supplied within a reasonable time.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies those rights.

  • For a major failure with a service, you may cancel the contract and obtain a refund for the unused portion, or seek compensation for the reduction in value below the price paid. You may also be entitled to compensation for other reasonably foreseeable loss.
  • For a failure that is not major, you are entitled to have the problem fixed within a reasonable time. If it is not fixed, you may cancel and obtain a refund for the unused portion.

A change of mind outside the stated window is not, by itself, a failure of a consumer guarantee. We offer the 14 day window voluntarily, in addition to those rights.

If we cannot resolve a dispute, you may contact your state or territory fair trading body, or the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission. See also section 15 of the Terms of Service.

10. How to request a refund

Email billing@fieldnotetravel.com.au with:

  • the email address on the account
  • the date and amount of the charge
  • a sentence on why you are asking, which helps us fix the underlying problem

You can also use the contact form and select Billing, subscriptions or refunds, or call +61 2 8000 0000 between 9:00 and 17:00 AEST, Monday to Friday.

We acknowledge within 2 business days and decide within 5.
