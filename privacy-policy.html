1. Who we are

In this policy, we, us and Fieldnote mean Fieldnote Travel Pty Ltd, ABN 00 000 000 000, of Suite 4, Level 2, 118 Kippax Street, Surry Hills NSW 2010, Australia.

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in Schedule 1 of that Act. This policy explains how we handle personal information, which the Act defines as information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable.

Our privacy contact is privacy@fieldnotetravel.com.au, or by post to the address above marked Attention: Privacy Officer.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect only what we need to operate the service. Depending on how you use the site, that may include:

Anonymity. Under APP 2, you may use the trip planner anonymously, without an account. In that mode nothing you type is sent to us. You cannot receive a paid service, a refund or support anonymously, because we cannot identify the account concerned.

3. How we collect it

  • Directly from you, when you create an account, save a trip, subscribe, complete a form or contact us.
  • Automatically, through server logs and, where you have consented, analytics cookies.
  • From third parties, in limited circumstances: our payment processor confirms whether a payment succeeded, and our email provider reports delivery outcomes such as bounces and unsubscribes.

Where it is reasonable and practicable, we collect personal information directly from you rather than from someone else.

4. Why we collect it, and what we do with it

We use personal information for the primary purpose for which it was collected, and for related secondary purposes you would reasonably expect. Specifically:

  • To create and administer your account and to save, share and export your trips
  • To process subscription payments, issue tax invoices and manage refunds and cancellations
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • To send service messages such as renewal notices, price change notices, security notices and receipts. These are not marketing and you cannot opt out of them while you hold an account
  • To send marketing emails, only where you have given express consent
  • To understand which pages and guides are useful, so we can improve them, using aggregated analytics where you have consented to analytics cookies
  • To measure the performance of our advertising, where you have consented to advertising cookies
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents
  • To comply with our legal obligations, including Australian tax and record-keeping law

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use three categories:

  • Essential cookies, which are necessary for the site to function, such as keeping you logged in and remembering your cookie choice. These are set without consent because the site cannot work without them.
  • Analytics cookies, which tell us in aggregate which pages are read and where people leave. These are set only with your consent.
  • Advertising cookies, including cookies used by Google, which allow us to measure the performance of advertising and, where applicable, to show more relevant advertising and to limit how often you see the same advertisement. These are set only with your consent.

Google's use of advertising cookies. Where you consent to advertising cookies, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to you based on your visit to our site or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising through the industry opt-out pages operated by the Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance.

No non-essential cookie is set before you choose in the consent banner. You may accept all, reject all non-essential cookies, or manage each category individually, and you may change your choice at any time. Rejecting analytics and advertising cookies does not reduce your access to any content on this site.

Full detail of each cookie category is in our Cookie Policy.

7. Overseas disclosure

Some of the service providers listed above operate outside Australia. Where personal information is disclosed to an overseas recipient we take reasonable steps, as required by APP 8, to ensure the recipient does not breach the Australian Privacy Principles in relation to that information, including by contract.

The countries in which recipients are likely to be located are the United States, the European Union and Singapore. Our primary application and database hosting is in Australia.

By providing personal information to us, you acknowledge that overseas recipients may not be subject to the Privacy Act, and that if an overseas recipient handles your information in breach of the Australian Privacy Principles, you may not be able to seek redress under the Act against that recipient.

8. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference and loss, and from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, as required by APP 11. Those steps include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS across the entire site
  • Encryption of data at rest in our database and backups
  • Passwords stored only as salted hashes, never in readable form
  • Access to production systems limited to staff who need it, with multi-factor authentication
  • Logging of administrative access
  • Regular patching of servers and dependencies
  • Contractual security obligations on our service providers

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your account password confidential.

9. Notifiable data breaches

The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act applies to us. If we have reasonable grounds to believe an eligible data breach has occurred, that is, unauthorised access to or disclosure of personal information, or loss of it, that is likely to result in serious harm to any individual whose information is involved, and we cannot prevent that harm through remedial action, we will:

  1. Contain the breach and assess it promptly, within 30 days of becoming aware of the grounds for suspicion
  2. Notify affected individuals as soon as practicable, describing the breach, the kinds of information involved and the steps we recommend they take
  3. Notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as soon as practicable

11. Direct marketing and the Spam Act

We send commercial electronic messages only in accordance with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). That means:

  • Consent. We rely on express consent, given by ticking an unticked box and confirming through a verification email. We keep a record of when and how consent was given.
  • Identification. Every marketing message clearly identifies Fieldnote Travel Pty Ltd as the sender and includes our ABN and contact details.
  • Unsubscribe. Every marketing message contains a functional unsubscribe facility that is honoured within five business days and at no cost to you.

Service messages relating to an account you hold, such as receipts, renewal notices and security alerts, are not marketing and are sent for as long as the account exists.

12. Access and correction

Under APP 12 and APP 13 you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.

To make a request, email privacy@fieldnotetravel.com.au or write to the Privacy Officer at the postal address above. We will:

  • Acknowledge your request within 5 business days
  • Verify your identity before releasing anything
  • Respond within 30 days
  • Provide the information in a reasonable format, at no charge for a straightforward request

If we refuse access or refuse to make a correction, we will tell you in writing why, and how you may complain.

You can also correct most account information yourself in your account settings, and you can delete your account at any time.

13. Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles, please tell us first. Email privacy@fieldnotetravel.com.au with the details. We will acknowledge within 5 business days, investigate, and respond in writing within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):

  • Online: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
  • Post: GPO Box 5218, Sydney NSW 2001

You do not need our permission to complain to the OAIC, and complaining to us first does not limit that right.

14. Children

The service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for, or knowingly collect personal information from, children under 15 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact our Privacy Officer and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version number and effective date at the top of this page will change, and material changes will be notified by email to account holders at least 14 days before they take effect. Continuing to use the service after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
