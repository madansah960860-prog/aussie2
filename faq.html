FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Grouped by topic. If your question is not here, the contact page has a form, an email address, a phone number and a postal address, all monitored by a real person.

01

Using the planner

02

Pricing, billing and refunds

Up to two saved trips, basic day-plan cards with time-stamped stops, daily and trip budget totals in Australian dollars, every destination guide and sample itinerary, and browser printing. There is no time limit and no card required. Guide pages show display advertising on the free tier.

Pro is $9.50 a month billed monthly, or $89 a year billed annually. Both prices are in Australian dollars and include GST, and a tax invoice is issued for every payment. Subscriptions renew automatically at the price then current. We email at least 14 days before an annual renewal and at least 30 days before any price change affecting you. Full detail on the Pricing page.

Account, then Billing, then Cancel Subscription. No phone call and no retention process. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, and you keep Pro access until then. Your trips are not deleted: if you have more than two, they become read-only until you delete some or resubscribe, and you can export any of them to PDF before cancelling.

A full refund is available within 14 days of a first Pro purchase or an annual renewal, provided you have not exported a PDF or used a sharing link in that period. The custom itinerary service has separate terms because work begins immediately. Everything is set out in the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Nothing in our terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

No, deliberately. The free tier is permanent rather than a countdown, so there is nothing that quietly converts into a charge on day fifteen. If you subscribe and change your mind, the refund window above applies.

03

Guides, prices and accuracy

They are estimates, built line by line for mid-range travel and stated per person on a twin-share basis, with the assumptions printed under each table. They are not quoted prices and we do not control them. Costs move with season, exchange rate and availability, so always check current prices with the provider before committing to anything.

Use it as a starting point, not as a determination. Our visa lines describe the general position for Australian passport holders travelling as tourists. Entry rules change without notice and vary with purpose of travel, length of stay and passport validity. Always check the destination government's immigration authority and the Smartraveller advisory before you book. See our Travel Advice Disclaimer.

Sometimes. Accommodation, flight, car hire and tour partners may pay us a commission if you book through a link on our site. Your price is not increased, and we are paid the same regardless of which option within a category you choose. Every affiliate link is labelled where it appears, and the Affiliate Disclosure explains the arrangements in full.

No. No advertiser or affiliate partner sees a guide before publication or can request a change to one. Commercial arrangements do not change the order destinations appear in and do not change what a guide says about a place. No property or operator has paid to be listed in a where-to-stay section.

Please tell us. Write to corrections@fieldnotetravel.com.au with the page and what is wrong. We respond within five business days, make the correction and update the last-updated date on the page.

04

Privacy, email and cookies

In line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. We collect only what we need to run the service, we do not sell personal information, and we tell you when information is disclosed to a third party such as a payment processor. The Privacy Policy sets out what is collected, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, overseas transfers, how to access or correct it, and how to complain to us or to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Only if you tick the newsletter box, which is never pre-ticked. Subscribing requires you to confirm by email before your address is added. Every marketing email identifies Fieldnote Travel Pty Ltd as the sender, includes our contact details, and carries a working unsubscribe link that we action within five business days, in line with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

Essential cookies that make the site work, and, only with your consent, analytics and advertising cookies including Google advertising cookies. No non-essential cookie is set before you choose in the banner. You can accept all, reject non-essential, or manage each category, and change your mind at any time. The Cookie Policy lists each category and what it does.

Yes. Email privacy@fieldnotetravel.com.au. We acknowledge requests within five business days and action them within 30 days. Deleting an account removes your trips and personal details, though we retain transaction records for the period required by Australian tax law.

05

Accessibility and technical

We build to WCAG 2.1 Level AA: semantic landmarks, a skip-to-content link, visible focus states, full keyboard operation, descriptive alt text on every image, and a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5 to 1 for body text. All motion respects the reduced-motion preference. If you encounter a barrier, email access@fieldnotetravel.com.au. We respond within five business days with a plan. See the Accessibility Statement.

No. There is no app, no extension and no download. The site never triggers an automatic download, never opens a pop-up window, and never redirects you somewhere you did not ask to go. The browser back button always works.

Yes, on any plan. Every itinerary page has a print button, and the site includes a print stylesheet that removes navigation, images, advertising and the cookie banner, leaving a clean day-by-day plan with the costs and travel times intact. Pro adds a formatted PDF export designed for offline use.
