IMAGE ASSET NOTES
-----------------------------------------
Not copy, but the display campaign will be rejected or under-served without
them. Prepare before launch:

Landscape image, 1.91:1, minimum 600 x 314    real travel photography, wide framing
Square image, 1:1, minimum 300 x 300          same photograph, cropped square
Portrait image, 4:5, minimum 480 x 600        optional, improves placement coverage
Logo, square 1:1, minimum 128 x 128           transparent background
Logo, landscape 4:1, minimum 512 x 128        transparent background

Image rules to follow:
- Real photographs only. No illustrations, no vector artwork, no generated
  graphics.
- No text overlaid on the image. Google penalises heavy text in display assets.
- No collages, no borders, no drop shadows, no watermarks.
- Natural light, wide framing, consistent 3:2 or 1.91:1 crops.
- Confirm the licence for every photograph before it is uploaded.
- Every image must be relevant to the landing page it serves.
