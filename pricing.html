Pricing and plans

All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. There is no free trial that converts automatically, no card required for the free tier, and every term that affects what you pay is stated on this page rather than buried in the Terms of Service.

03

Billing, cancellation and refunds

Stated here in full, not only in the Terms of Service.

  • Currency and taxAll prices are in Australian dollars and include GST at 10 per cent. A tax invoice is issued for every payment.
  • Billing frequencyPro is charged either monthly on the anniversary of the day you subscribed, or annually on the anniversary of the date you subscribed. The custom itinerary service is a single payment taken when you accept the brief.
  • Payment methodsVisa, Mastercard and American Express. Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider; we do not store your full card number.
  • Automatic renewalPro renews automatically at the end of each period at the price then current for your plan. We email you at least 14 days before an annual renewal, and at least 30 days before any price change affecting you.
  • CancellingCancel at any time from Account, Billing, Cancel Subscription. No phone call, no retention process. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for and you keep Pro access until then.
  • What happens to your tripsTrips are not deleted when Pro ends. If you have more than two saved trips, they become read-only until you either delete some or resubscribe. You can export any trip to PDF before cancelling.
  • Refund windowA full refund is available within 14 days of a first Pro purchase or an annual renewal if you have not exported a PDF or used a sharing link in that period. Full detail is in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • Your consumer rightsNothing in our terms limits rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law, including remedies where a service is not supplied with due care and skill or does not match its description.

05

How we make money

Subscriptions are the part we want to grow. The rest is disclosed so you can weigh it.

  • Pro subscriptionsThe prices on this page. Our main revenue line, and the one that does not depend on you booking anything.
  • Affiliate commissionsAccommodation, flight, car hire and tour partners may pay us a commission if you book through a link on our site. Your price is not increased. Every link is labelled at the point it appears. See the Affiliate Disclosure.
  • Paid custom itinerariesThe one-time $189 service above.
  • Agent and white-label plansThe business plans above.
  • Travel insurance referralsAn optional add-on. If we refer you to a licensed insurance partner we may be paid a referral fee and will say so at the link. We are not licensed to give financial advice and do not recommend policies. See the insurance guide.
  • Display advertisingLabelled ad slots on guide and blog pages only. Never on legal pages, never above the main content of a page, and not shown to Pro accounts.

What commercial arrangements never do: change the order destinations appear in, change what a guide says about a place, or introduce urgency that is not real. We do not run countdown timers, and we do not display room-scarcity messages.
