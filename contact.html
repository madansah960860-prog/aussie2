04

What we cannot help with

Being clear about this saves everyone time.

  • Bookings. We are not a travel agent and we do not make, change or cancel bookings. Contact the airline, hotel or operator you booked with.
  • Visa determinations. We cannot tell you whether you will be granted a visa. Contact the destination government's immigration authority or a registered migration agent.
  • Insurance advice. We do not hold an Australian Financial Services Licence and cannot recommend a policy or advise on a claim. Speak to a licensed adviser or broker.
  • Consular assistance. If you are in difficulty overseas, contact the nearest Australian embassy, high commission or consulate, or the Consular Emergency Centre.
  • Medical advice. Speak to a GP or a travel health clinic.
