04

Getting a realistic day out of the planner

Four habits that separate a plan that survives contact with a real holiday from one that does not.

Put the travel time in first

Before you add attractions, add the driving, the ferry and the train. A day with two hours of transport has room for two stops, not five. Most over-planned days are transport days in disguise.

Cost the boring things

Parking, coffee, an entry fee, a second coffee, dinner. Individually small, collectively the difference between a $120 day and a $210 day. The boring line items are where budgets actually go.

Leave one afternoon per three days empty

Weather, illness, a place you liked more than expected. If every afternoon is booked, the first thing that goes wrong ruins the rest of the plan.

Plan arrival day as a half day

An overnight flight from the east coast lands you functional but not sharp. Book accommodation you can walk to, eat early, and start properly the next morning.
