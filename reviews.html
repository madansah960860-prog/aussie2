Reviews

Reviews

Every review below was submitted by a Fieldnote account holder after a trip was marked complete, and is published with that person's written permission. We do not edit wording, we do not remove critical reviews, and we do not pay for reviews or offer anything in exchange for one.

01

How we collect reviews

Stated first, because a review page without a method is just marketing.

  1. The invitation

    Two weeks after a trip's end date passes, account holders receive one email inviting them to review the planner. One email only. There is no reminder sequence and no incentive attached.

  2. Verification

    Reviews can only be submitted from a logged-in account with a completed trip. Anonymous submissions from the public web are not accepted, which is why you cannot leave a review on this page.

  3. Publication

    We ask separately for permission to publish, along with a first name, last initial, suburb and state. Nothing is published without that. Reviewers can withdraw permission at any time and we remove the review within five business days.

  4. What we do not do

    We do not write reviews ourselves, buy them, incentivise them, filter out low ratings, or ask people to change what they wrote. Critical reviews appear on this page alongside the rest, because a page of five-star ratings tells you nothing.

03

What we did about the criticism

Two of the reviews above are not positive. Here is what happened next.

On mobile use while travelling

Hamish is right that the planner is built for the desk rather than the footpath. Our position for now is that the PDF export and the print stylesheet are the correct answers while you are actually on a trip, because they work with no signal and no battery anxiety. A read-only mobile view of a saved trip is on the list.

On the free tier limit

Aled is also right, and this one was our fault. The two-trip limit is now stated on the home page, in the planner sidebar, on the Pricing page and in the comparison table, rather than only appearing when you hit it. We do not think a limit is unfair; hiding it is.

04

Can I leave a review?

Only if you hold an account and have completed a trip in the planner. That restriction exists so that everything on this page is traceable to a real user, which matters more to us than having more reviews.

If you have used Fieldnote and want to tell us something, whether or not it is publishable, the contact form reaches the same inbox. Feedback about the guides themselves goes to corrections@fieldnotetravel.com.au.

If you believe a review on this page is inaccurate or should not have been published, tell us and we will investigate. We would rather remove a review we cannot stand behind than defend one.

Form your own view

The planner is free to try, with no account and no card. Reviews are useful; using the thing is better.
