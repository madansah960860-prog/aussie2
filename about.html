About

An Australian trip planner, written from here

We are a small company in Sydney building the planning tool and the destination guides we wanted when we were planning our own trips.

See how the planner works Contact us
01

Why this exists

Almost every travel planning tool available to Australians is written somewhere else. That sounds like a small thing until you are trying to work out whether November is a good month, and the guide you are reading was written by someone for whom November means the start of winter.

The same gap runs through everything. Prices quoted in other currencies. Flight times measured from London. School holiday advice that does not know Australia has eight different sets of them. Visa guidance written for a passport that is not the one in your drawer. None of it is wrong, exactly. It is just written for someone else.

Fieldnote is the correction. Every season is written against the Southern Hemisphere calendar. Every price is in Australian dollars. Every flight time is measured from Sydney, with notes for Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth. Every visa line describes the position for Australian passport holders, with a link to the official source. Every date is DD/MM/YYYY and every distance is in kilometres.

The second thing we wanted was a planner that admitted travel takes time. Most itineraries online are lists of attractions with no sense of how long it takes to get between them, which is why so many holiday days collapse at four in the afternoon. Every Fieldnote day card carries the travel time in the gap between stops, and a cost for the day underneath. Those two numbers are the whole idea.

We are not a travel agent. We do not sell flights, accommodation or tours, we do not take bookings, and we do not hold client money for travel arrangements. We are not an insurer or a licensed financial adviser. We are not a booking comparison site.

03

How the guides get written

Every destination guide follows the same process, and we would rather publish four good ones than forty thin ones.

  1. Research against primary sources

    Seasons come from meteorological records rather than marketing copy. Visa and entry lines come from the destination government's own immigration authority and are checked against Smartraveller. Transport times come from operator timetables and, where we can, from having done the drive.

  2. Costs built from the bottom up

    Daily budget tables are assembled line by line, per person, on a twin-share basis, and the assumptions are printed underneath the table. When we do not know, we say so rather than inventing a number.

  3. Reviewed against a real day

    Before a sample plan is published, someone checks that the day is physically possible: that the drive fits, that the site is open at that hour, that the walk takes as long as we say it does.

  4. Dated and corrected

    Every guide carries a last-updated date. When something changes, we change it and note it. Corrections go to corrections@fieldnotetravel.com.au and we would genuinely rather hear from you than be wrong in public.

How we make money

The same six lines appear on the home page, the How It Works page and the Pricing page, because it should not take effort to find them.

  • Pro subscriptions$9.50 a month or $89 a year in AUD. Our main revenue and the part we want to grow, because it does not depend on you booking anything.
  • Affiliate commissionsAccommodation, flight, car hire and tour partners may pay us a commission if you book through a link on our site. Your price does not go up. Every link is labelled where it appears. See the Affiliate Disclosure.
  • Paid custom itinerariesA one-time $189 fee for a plan written and revised by a human planner.
  • Agent and white-label plansMonthly subscriptions for Australian travel agents and small tour operators. See For Travel Agents.
  • Travel insurance referralsAn optional add-on. We may be paid a referral fee, and we say so at the link. We do not hold an Australian Financial Services Licence and do not recommend policies.
  • Display advertisingLabelled ad slots on guide and blog pages only. Never on legal pages, never above the main content, and not shown to Pro accounts.

The line we hold

Commercial arrangements do not change the order destinations appear in, do not change what a guide says about a place, and do not add urgency that is not real. No advertiser or affiliate partner sees a guide before publication or can request a change to one. We do not run countdown timers or scarcity messages, because they are not true.

What we will not do

  • Publish a review we did not receive from a verified account holder
  • Remove a critical review because it is inconvenient
  • Claim to be the best, the cheapest or number one at anything
  • Show a fake countdown, a fake room count or invented urgency
  • Sell your personal information
  • Set an analytics or advertising cookie before you consent
  • Email you marketing without your express consent, or without a working unsubscribe
  • Give you personal financial, medical, legal or migration advice

If we fall short of any of that, tell us. The contact page lists a form, an email address, a phone number and a postal address, and a real person reads all four.
