How these guides are written and maintained

Editorial standards matter more on this part of the site than anywhere else, because these pages carry advertising.

Advertising does not touch the copy

Guide pages carry display advertising, and some contain affiliate links. No advertiser or affiliate partner sees a guide before it is published, and none of them can request a change to one. Where a guide mentions a category of product, it does so because the category is relevant, not because a partner is paying.

Affiliate links are labelled where they appear

Not just on a disclosure page you have to go looking for. Every guide that contains an affiliate link carries a disclosure block near that link, consistent with Australian Consumer Law and ACCC guidance on disclosing commercial relationships.

Updates and corrections

Each guide shows a last-updated date in DD/MM/YYYY. Where information changes frequently, such as visa rules or telecommunications pricing, the guide points at the official source rather than repeating a figure that will be wrong in six months.

If you find something inaccurate, tell us. Corrections are made and noted. Write to corrections@fieldnotetravel.com.au.

What we do not do

We do not give personal financial, medical, legal or migration advice. Where a subject is regulated, such as insurance, we describe how the category works in general terms and direct you to a licensed adviser and to the product disclosure statement.
