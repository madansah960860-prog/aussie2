01

Who this is for

Small and independent Australian travel businesses that produce written itineraries for clients: home-based agents, boutique agencies, small group tour operators, corporate travel arrangers and DMC representatives.

Independent agents

You are the whole business. The itinerary document is your product as much as the booking is, and it currently lives in a template you have been patching for four years.

Boutique agencies

Three to ten consultants, each with their own version of the same Japan document. A shared template library ends that.

Small tour operators

You run a handful of departures a year and need a clean, costed day plan to send to guests, with your logo on it and no mention of ours.

02

What the business plans add

Everything in Pro, plus the parts that only matter when the document is going to a client.

  • Branded PDF exportYour logo, your colours, your contact block and your terms on the last page. No Fieldnote branding on Agency and White Label plans.
  • Shared template librarySave any itinerary as a team template. New enquiries start from a real plan rather than a blank page.
  • Client sharing linksSend a read-only web link that stays current when you edit the plan, instead of emailing a fourth PDF revision.
  • Cost lines you controlShow or hide per-item costs, show a total only, or hide costs entirely for a document that sits alongside your quote.
  • Multiple seatsConsultants have their own logins and their own client lists, with a shared template pool.
  • Team activity logSee who changed what and when. Useful when a client rings about a version they were sent last week.
  • Bulk duplicateCopy a departure and shift every date by a fixed number of days. A recurring tour becomes one build a year, not twelve.
  • Australian supportEmail support during Australian business hours, and a named contact on White Label.

What we are not

Fieldnote is an itinerary building tool. We are not a booking platform, not a wholesaler, not a GDS and not a payments provider. We do not touch your bookings, your commissions or your client's money, and we do not sell travel. Your relationship with your clients and your suppliers stays entirely yours.
